About Us

Traditional technique, modern taste

Udon House began with a simple idea: handmade noodles, clean broths, and honest ingredients. We source local produce and prepare each bowl by hand. Visit us for a quick lunch or a relaxed dinner.

Our chefs have trained in traditional Japanese techniques, bringing authentic flavors to every bowl. We believe in the power of simple, high-quality ingredients to create memorable dining experiences.
